application.svg and chevron-down.svg
  Original generic artwork for this project, Apache-2.0; see ../LICENSE.
  The simple chevron menu mark was redrawn on 2026-11-11 without third-party
  artwork; it is not claimed to be an application brand icon.
  application.svg represents VS Code, Finder, Zed and IntelliJ IDEA neutrally
  when no local icon is cached, and is the fallback for custom applications.
  macOS installation exports the four icons from locally installed apps to

  This local cache does not establish redistribution permission.
  All application names, bundle IDs and launch choices are preserved.
